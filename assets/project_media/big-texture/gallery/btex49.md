The empire crumbles, and here the metanarrative ends. The end of the second age of television, and now it is clear that we will never recreate the pre-blast world, we will never reach the other continents. I hear there are attempts to reinstate the Archivists, but the Deer Boy is a hero, not a leader. I don't think I'll see any more in my lifetime, but who cares about *my* life time? I think of the last days I spent out at sea, watching Nemon and the others dive in to the home of the whales. Goodbye, goodbye... how I miss you all.
