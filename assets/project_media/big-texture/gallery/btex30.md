**Xylem Tube** will look out into the distance wistfully these days if you ask her about **Sir Gabriel Peppers**. She will tell you that the last she heard of him, he was headed up to Polar with a pack of dogs, and how she plans to follow one day with her daughters and, if possible, the Deer Boy, to meet him, to look him in the eyes once more as her dearest friend, before either of them must return to the ground. She will never make it there.
