**Xylem Tube's** immaculate collection of paperbacks and notebooks starts with a pre-blast gift from her aunt she must've been far too young for - **Ashton Dress'** satirical western Lobofield Newportia. It is cynical and at times, excessive to the point of ham-fistedness, but she loves it. She pulls it, dusty and singed, from the wreckage of her apartment building, and hobbles to the west.
