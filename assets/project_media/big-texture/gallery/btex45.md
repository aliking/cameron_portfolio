The Archivist libraries are burnt shortly into the Second Age of Television, as their forces are slaughtered. In a broadcast, **Grand Suzerain Cecelia Television** says that she does not believe in the Church of the Zero State's gods, and so she will not respect their right to exist. The latter statement has been proven to be true, but the validity of her first claim is largely disputed. It is said that when she was told that the Diary of Alma Wheeler had not been found and burned, she nearly fainted from stress.
