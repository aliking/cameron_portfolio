I have a dream of my old friend, the **Deer Boy**, who has since evolved into a creature half-human, half story. He is an Archivist product, and I oftentimes wonder if he ever really did the things they say he did. Then I think of him that summer, the one time he joined us out to sea just for the fun of it, and I realize that every story is most certainly possible. That when I dream of him, in the woods of Cascadia, killing that fifth angel, I have no reason to believe this dream is not true. Tonight, as I pray for the first time, I decide to speak out into the void, to the Zero State, to ask my question.
in response, one word: "Alma."
