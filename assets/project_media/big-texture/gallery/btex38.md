Children are often touched by the ways of **Ms. Lavani**, though the Archivists she trained to eventually replace her in her later years often lack this gift. it takes a talent mind, a true reader, to resist the ideological pull of a new cultural wave. Soon, Lavani's interest in developing the Archivist role into an organization and her past affiliations with the Church of the Zero State began to intersect, leading to the downfall of both factions.
