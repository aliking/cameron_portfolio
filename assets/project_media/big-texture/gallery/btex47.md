Lavani and Sir Gabriel both say they waited outside of the church, that the **Deer Boy** was the only one who entered, and he... he will never say what he saw in there, what he heard. Sir Gabriel's reports later offer just one small piece of information - that when he led the boy in, he saw, filling the pews, dozens and dozens of people, all dressed the same, all covering one eye, all silent and staring. Little is known of the Church of the Zero State beforehand, and after they sneakily merged with the Archivists and were wiped out, nobody knows what their eventual true goals were.
