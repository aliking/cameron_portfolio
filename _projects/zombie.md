Following the 2036 bombing at Gorowitz Law's Manhattan Office and the disappearance of managing partner Louis Cohen, investigators began to look into his past, discovering a number of bombings that took place in his senior year of high school in Spenville, Illinois. 

The testimony of Gillian Crosby, Louis' classmate, paints a portrait of the mercurial life these teenagers lived, under constant fear of fiery death. Such a death has already begun to claim a few lives...
