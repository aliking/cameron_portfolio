In the not-so-distant future, following Senator Roswell Geiger's North American Nuclear Holocaust, all civilization and land in the United States, Canada, and some parts of Mexico have regressed to a fantastical wilderness. The people are rancid-skinned, sickly, and their infastructure has been reduced to nothing but a *big texture* of rubble over the land.

The American myth chokes on its own frothy saliva every night, born again just in tip to wipe its mouth and shoot up again. Irony does not exist in this New Country, which is now beginning to reform into something medieval and magical, a truly American land of American Quests and Knightly Orders of America. Here, see the form of the landscape in the radiation-addled eyes of its inhabitants...
