Featuring various pieces of art, film, and design - mainly related to **Tales From The Blindspot** (also known as *A Sophomore Effort*), an surrealist examination of an alternate American 21st Century, that follows the small lives of individuals goign through their boring, monotonous lives that often spiral into major cosmic events, all while the American Dynasty crumbles around them into nothing but pulverized rock and *big texture*.
